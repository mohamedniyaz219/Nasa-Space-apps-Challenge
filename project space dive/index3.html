Mercury

The smallest and innermost planet, Mercury has an eccentric orbit and extreme temperature variations.

Venus

Known as Earth's "sister planet," Venus has a thick atmosphere and is the hottest planet in the solar system.

Earth

The third planet from the Sun, Earth is the only known celestial body to support life.

Mars

Known as the "Red Planet," Mars has the tallest volcano and deepest canyon in the solar system.

Jupiter

The largest planet, Jupiter is a gas giant with a Great Red Spot, a massive storm larger than Earth.

Saturn

Best known for its stunning rings, Saturn is another gas giant made mainly of hydrogen and helium.

Uranus

An ice giant, Uranus rotates on its side and has a blue-green hue due to methane in its atmosphere.

Neptune

Another ice giant, Neptune has strong winds and storm systems, including the Great Dark Spot.

Pluto

Once the ninth planet, Pluto is now classified as a dwarf planet and is part of the Kuiper Belt.

A bright comet that became visible to the naked eye in July 2020, discovered by the NEOWISE spacecraft.

One of the most observed comets of the 20th century, visible for a record 18 months in 1996-1997.

Comet Hyakutake (C/1996 B2)

Comet Shoemaker-Levy 9
